Academics

Curriculum Overview

At [SCHOOL], our curriculum is designed to inspire curiosity, develop critical thinking, and build a strong foundation for lifelong learning. We follow a standards-aligned framework enhanced by project-based learning, interdisciplinary studies, and real-world application.

Grade Levels

Each division is thoughtfully designed to meet the developmental needs of students at every stage.

Grades K – 5

Lower School

Foundational skills in literacy, mathematics, science, and social studies are paired with art, music, Spanish, and physical education. Emphasis on social-emotional learning and building a love for school.

Grades 6 – 8

Middle School

Students rotate through core subjects with specialist teachers while exploring electives: coding, world languages, band, visual arts, and design thinking. Advisory program supports the middle years transition.

Grades 9 – 12

Upper School

College-preparatory program with 14 AP courses, honors tracks, dual-enrollment options, and a senior capstone research project. 100% of graduates are accepted to four-year universities.

Special Programs

🔬

1:1 device program from Grade 3, digital literacy, and responsible tech use.

❤️

Service Learning

Grade-level partnerships with local nonprofits and an annual all-school day of service.

Student Support

We meet every learner where they are. Our team includes learning specialists, school counselors, a literacy coach, and a math interventionist. Whether a student needs enrichment, accommodations, or just a trusted adult to talk to, support is always available.

Our college counseling program begins in Grade 9 with individualized guidance through course selection, standardized testing, essay writing, and the application process.
