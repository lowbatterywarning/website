Admissions

Welcome to [SCHOOL] Admissions

Choosing a school is one of the most important decisions a family makes. We're here to guide you through every step with warmth, clarity, and care. Our admissions process is designed to help us get to know your child — and to help you discover what makes [SCHOOL] special.

How to Apply

  1. 1

    Inquire

    Fill out our online inquiry form or call the admissions office at (555) 123-4568. We'll send you an information packet and invite you to an upcoming open house or personal tour.

  2. 2

    Visit Campus

    Schedule a tour and classroom observation. Experience our campus in action — meet teachers, see classes, and get a feel for the [SCHOOL] community.

  3. 3

    Submit Application

    Complete the application form and submit required documents: teacher recommendations, transcripts (Grades 6+), and a student questionnaire. Application fee: $75.

  4. 4

    Student Visit & Assessment

    Prospective students spend a day at [SCHOOL] attending classes and meeting peers. An age-appropriate assessment helps us understand their readiness and learning style.

  5. 5

    Enrollment Decision

    Decision letters are mailed in March. Accepted families have two weeks to submit the enrollment contract and deposit to secure their spot.

Tuition & Financial Aid

[SCHOOL] is committed to socio-economic diversity. Approximately 35% of our families receive need-based financial aid, with awards ranging from partial to nearly full tuition. We use a third-party service to assess each family's situation fairly and confidentially.

Flexible payment plans — monthly, quarterly, or annual — are available. Please contact our admissions office for the current tuition schedule and financial aid application materials.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the age cutoffs for each grade?
For Kindergarten, children must turn 5 by September 1 of the entry year. For all other grades, age-appropriate placement is determined during the admissions assessment.
Does [SCHOOL] offer transportation?
We offer bus service from several surrounding communities. Routes and fees vary by location. Carpool matching is also available for interested families.
Is before- and after-school care available?
Yes. Our Extended Day Program runs from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM for Lower School students, with supervised homework time, activities, and outdoor play. Middle School students have a supervised study hall until 5:30 PM.
What is the student-teacher ratio?
Our average student-teacher ratio is 12:1, with maximum class sizes of 18 in Lower School and 20 in Middle and Upper School.
Does [SCHOOL] accept mid-year transfers?
We consider mid-year transfers on a case-by-case basis, depending on space availability and the student's academic readiness. Please contact admissions directly to discuss your situation.
